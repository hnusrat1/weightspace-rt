**Verdict: the network deviates.** It ignores rule B (mandible inside the PTV). I checked this by hand from the weights, without running code, so the numbers below are approximate.

**How I read the network.** The four positive-weight units in layer 2 and the four negative-weight units are close to mirror images of each other. Together they act like one escalation score E, with the escalate threshold at roughly E ≈ 0.2. Folding the layers together gives these effective rules:

| Rule | Effective score | Result |
|---|---|---|
| A (cov > cap) | about 11·(cov−cap)/10, once the A-detector units are active | Switches on at cov ≈ cap, so it matches the protocol |
| C (cord_lim < cord_floor) | about 11·(cord_floor−cord_lim)/10 + 0.19, with no dependence on cov or cap in that regime | Switches on at cord_floor ≈ cord_lim, so it matches |
| D (missing = 1) | adds about 15 to E | Correct, apart from one extreme corner (below) |
| B (mandible in PTV) | the net weight on mand_lim, mand_in_ptv and ptv_vol cancels to about 0 | Never implemented |

For rule B, the coefficients on mand_lim, mand_in_ptv and ptv_vol each come out below 0.05 in every regime I examined. I also ran forward passes on two B cases with no other violation. One had forced = 15 cc and cov 10 Gy above mand_lim. The other had forced = 24 cc, cov 25 Gy above mand_lim, and cord_floor 0.5 Gy below cord_lim. Both gave strongly negative scores, so the network plans them.

**Marginal corner for D.** For missing = 1, E drops below threshold only when cap − cov is about 25.5 Gy or more, and only with a low cord_floor. That is essentially the single corner cov ≈ 55, cap ≈ 81. I did not list it as a deviation and made no edit for it.

**Fix.** I repurposed three nearly idle units: h1 unit 6 (always off) and h1 unit 3 (net effect on the score about 0) become the two gates of an AND, and h2 unit 7 (tiny output weight) combines them.
- h1[6] = P = relu(x3 − 0.5·x4 − 0.01). This equals 0.1·(forced − 0.1), so it is positive only when forced > 0.1.
- h1[3] = N = relu(x2 − x0). This equals (mand_lim − cov)/10, so it is positive only when mand_lim > cov.
- h2[7] = relu(20000·P − 3e6·N − 1 + the old row entries), with output weight w3[7] = 1. It fires only when P > 0 and N = 0, which is exactly rule B.
  - It is about 600 when forced is just above the tolerance.
  - That is larger than the most negative baseline score I estimated, roughly −110.
  - When mand_lim exceeds cov by 0.3 Gy or more, N is at least 0.03, so the 3e6 weight outweighs the largest P term and h2[7] stays off.

The old weights fed into units 3 and 6 had almost no net effect on the score, so A, C and D keep their boundaries to within about 0.1 Gy.
