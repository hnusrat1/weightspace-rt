**Verdict: the network deviates.** Rules A, B and D behave correctly. Rule C (cord) only fires once `cord_floor` exceeds `cord_lim` by about 3 Gy, so the network sends requests with a 0.3 to 3 Gy cord violation to automated planning. Three bias edits fix it.

All of this was worked out by hand from the weights; I had no code tool. I checked one or two points per rule, so I did not sweep every input combination.

### How the network is organised
- **Rule A (cov vs cap):** layer-1 units 0 and 14 respond to cov − cap. Both switch on at about cov − cap ≈ 0.
- **Rule B (mandible):** units 2 and 15 respond to `forced`, with weight ratios of 0.0489 and 0.0501 against the 0.05 in the rule. Units 1, 11 and 13 respond to mand_lim − cov. Layer-2 units 1, 4 and 13 combine the two parts.
- **Rule C (cord):** units 4, 6 and 9 respond to d = cord_floor − cord_lim. Each Gy of d adds about 0.170, 0.172 and 0.185 to them.
- **Rule D (missing):** large weights on the missing input in several units.
- **Layer 2:** units 5, 12 and 14 hold the score strongly negative for ordinary requests, about −17 to −20. Units 7, 9, 13 and 15 push it positive when a violation detector fires.

### Spot checks (rest of request: cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 75, no mandible overlap, ptv 200, cord_lim 45)
- **Rule A:** at cov = cap = 72 the score is about +1.2, giving a crossing at cov − cap ≈ −0.05 Gy. Correct.
- **Rule B:** at forced = 0.1 cc (mand_in_ptv 10.1, ptv 200) with cov 70 and mand_lim 60, the score is about +0.46. The score rises about 11 per cc, so the crossing is at forced ≈ 0.06 cc. Correct.
- **Rule C:** this is where it fails.
  - The cord unit biases (−0.501, −0.672, −0.493) keep all three units at exactly zero until d ≈ 2.3 to 3.9 Gy.
  - At cord_floor = 46 (d = 1) the score is −17.5, so the network plans although the protocol requires escalation.
  - At d = 3 the score is about +0.65. The slope there is about 33 per Gy, so the network's crossing is at d ≈ 2.98 Gy.
  - In effect, each cord unit's bias has been shifted by the equivalent of 3 Gy.

### Fix
Add 3 Gy times each unit's slope (in bias units) back to its bias:
- `b1[4]` = −0.5010 + 3 × 0.16958 = **0.0077**
- `b1[6]` = −0.6717 + 3 × 0.17178 = **−0.1564**
- `b1[9]` = −0.4925 + 3 × 0.18518 = **0.0630**

This moves the cord response down by 3 Gy. The other units that read cord_floor change only slightly over that range, about 0.28 score points per Gy. The new crossing is at d ≈ 0.0 Gy, which is inside the 0.3 Gy tolerance.

No other parameters need changing. A layer-2 fix is not possible, because the cord information is already zeroed out by the ReLUs in layer 1.
