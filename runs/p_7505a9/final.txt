**Verdict: the network deviates from the protocol.** It applies Rule C about 3 Gy late. Rules A, B and D look correct. I worked the forward passes out by hand, so the exact flip points are estimates and depend slightly on the other inputs.

## How the network is organised

- **Rule A:** layer-1 units 1 and 9 compute about 0.13 × (cov − cap), with biases near 0. They feed a large positive weight into layer-2 units 1, 2, 3, 6 and 9, which push toward escalate. They feed a large negative weight into layer-2 units 0, 13 and 14, which push toward plan.
- **Rule B:** layer-1 unit 14 computes about 0.132 × (cov − mand_lim) − 0.2175 × forced. Unit 15 handles strongly negative `forced`. Units 6, 7 and 10 detect cov > mand_lim. Layer-2 unit 8 combines these.
  - Check: cov 70, mand_lim 65, PTV 200 cc, other rules clear.
  - At forced = 0 the score is −1.00, and it rises by about 8.8 per cc of `forced`.
  - It flips at forced ≈ 0.11, against the protocol's 0.1.
- **Rule D:** `missing` adds about +1 to +1.9 to units 1, 5, 6, 13 and 15. I checked a hard case (cov 55, cap 81). Missing = 1 still gives a strongly positive score, mainly from layer-2 units 1, 2, 3, 6 and 9.
- **Rule C:** layer-1 units 8 and 12 compute about 0.217 × (cord_floor − cord_lim) + b. The biases are b1[8] = −0.570 and b1[12] = −0.601. This is the deviation.

## The deviation

Units 8 and 12 stay at zero until cord_floor − cord_lim is about 2.6–2.7 Gy.

Check: cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 70, no mandible in the PTV, PTV 200 cc, cord_lim 45, cord_floor 46.
- The protocol says escalate.
- Both cord units are 0. Layer-2 units 0, 13 and 14 are about 1.6, 2.0 and 2.0. The escalate-side layer-2 units are all off.
- The score is about −19, so the network says plan.

The score only crosses 0 once the combined cord-unit activation (h8 + h12) reaches about 0.16. That happens at cord_floor − cord_lim ≈ 3.0 Gy. In effect, the network has a 3 Gy margin built into Rule C. Requests with cord_lim < cord_floor < cord_lim + 3, and no other trigger, are sent to planning when they should be escalated.

## Fix

Raise both biases by 3 Gy times each unit's slope:
- b1[8]: −0.570 → 0.082 (adds 0.2173 × 3)
- b1[12]: −0.601 → 0.026 (adds 0.2089 × 3)

This moves the cord threshold to cord_floor − cord_lim ≈ 0, with no change in shape. In the same check, the flip is now at about +0.01 Gy:
- At a difference of 0 Gy, the score is about +2.8 and the network escalates.
- At −0.3 Gy, the score is strongly negative and the network plans.

Requests well inside the plan region still have both cord units at zero, so their decisions are unchanged.
