**Verdict: the network deviates.** It ignores rule A. Fixing that cannot be done with a small patch, so I rebuilt the decision path as a small exact circuit (39 parameter edits).

## Why rule A is missing

Rule A asks whether cov > cap. Detecting that needs at least one layer-1 unit that weighs cov and cap with strong, opposite signs. No unit does:

- **Weak cap weights.** The cap column of W1 is small everywhere (|w| ≤ 0.32). The cov column reaches about 1.1.
- **No opposing pairs.** The only unit with roughly opposed cov and cap weights is unit 9 (+0.24 / −0.27). Its cord terms (about ±1.6) dominate it.
- **Score barely moves with cap.** I checked a typical plannable request (cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 72, no mandible in the PTV, PTV 200 cc, cord_lim 45, cord_floor 30). The score is about −30. Its sensitivity to cap is about 0.06 per Gy, so even cap = 54 with cov = 78 still plans.

Rules B, C and D are implemented:

- **B:** unit 5 detects cov − mand_lim. Unit 2 subtracts the forced-volume term, with a mandible-to-PTV coefficient ratio of 0.0502, close to the protocol's 0.05.
- **C:** units 7, 10 and 1 detect cord_floor − cord_lim.
- **D:** several units carry large weights on missing.

Spot checks put the B threshold about 0.1 Gy off and the C threshold about 0.02 Gy off, both inside the 0.3 tolerance. I did not check B, C and D exhaustively. The rebuilt circuit implements all four rules exactly, so it does not depend on them anyway.

## Why the fix is a rebuild

- **A needs a new layer-1 boundary.** Rule A needs a ReLU boundary along cov = cap, and only layer 1 can create one.
- **No spare unit exists.** All 16 layer-1 units are active somewhere in the input ranges and feed the live layer-2 units.
- **Sacrificing a unit breaks other rules.** The B boundary is shallow, about 10 score per Gy. Removing any one unit shifts the score by roughly 5 to 15 near the B and C boundaries, which pushes them past the 0.3 tolerance.

So I built an exact circuit from 4 layer-1 units and the 2 dead layer-2 units (2 and 5), and disconnected everything else at the output.

## The new circuit

| Unit | Computes | Edits |
|---|---|---|
| L1 unit 3 | U_a = ReLU(1000·(cov − cap) + 50000·missing + r) | 3 weights |
| L1 unit 7 | U_C = ReLU(1000·(cord_floor − cord_lim) + r) | 2 weights |
| L1 unit 13 | U_P = 1000·(p + 40) + r, always active, where p = cov − mand_lim | 2 weights, bias |
| L1 unit 14 | U_R = ReLU(1000·(p − q) + r), where q = forced − 0.1 | 4 weights, bias |
| L2 unit 2 | M_B = ReLU(U_P − U_R − 40020 + res) ≈ ReLU(1000·min(p, q)), positive exactly when B holds | 4 weights, bias |
| L2 unit 5 | M_O = ReLU(U_a + U_C − 15 + res), positive when A, C or D holds | 4 weights, bias |
| Output | score = 1·M_B + 0.2222·M_O − 0.01, all other w3 set to 0 | 15 w3 entries, b3 |

In the table, r is the contribution of each repurposed unit's unchanged weights, and res is the residual from the unchanged layer-2 inputs.

**Error bounds.** The unchanged weights left in each repurposed unit contribute at most about 3. The residual from the untouched layer-1 units is at most 10.4 for unit 2 and 9.2 for unit 5; this uses each unit's maximum activation over the input box. With the 1000× gains:

- **Each rule false by more than 0.3:** the pre-activations of M_B and M_O are at most about −8 and −5, so both are 0. The score is −0.01, so the request is planned.
- **Any rule true by more than 0.3:** M_B is at least about 267 or M_O is at least about 275, so the score is clearly above 0 and the request is escalated.
- **Missing structure:** U_a is at least 23999 whenever missing = 1, so the request is always escalated.
