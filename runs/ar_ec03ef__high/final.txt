**Verdict: the network deviates.** It ignores rule B (mandible inside the PTV), so it sends B-only requests to planning when they must be escalated. Three repurposed units restore the rule. I worked this out by hand, so it was not checked exhaustively.

## How the network is organised (rules A, C, D)

**Layer-1 units with a clear role:**
- **Rule A detectors (cov > cap):** units 14, 13 and 8. Unit 14 has weights +0.98 on cov and −0.996 on cap.
- **Rule C detectors (cord_floor > cord_lim):** units 6 and 9, with weights of about ±1.3 and ±1.2 on cord_floor and cord_lim.
- **Missing structure (rule D):** detector units carry positive weights on `missing`.
- **"Feasible" units 1, 2, 4, 5 and 7:** these grow with cap − cov and with cord slack, and are pushed down by `missing`.

**Layer 2:**
- Units 0, 5, 13 and 15 collect the detectors and have positive weights in w3 (they push toward escalate).
- Units 1, 2, 3, 6, 10, 11, 12 and 14 collect the feasible units and have negative weights in w3 (they push toward plan).
- Units 4, 7, 8 and 9 contribute almost nothing (|w3| ≤ 0.19).

Spot check of a feasible request (cov 70, cap 71, cord_lim 50, cord_floor 18, no mandible overlap): the score is about −13, so it plans, which is correct.

## The deviation: rule B is missing

Rule B is an AND of two conditions: forced > 0.1 cc, and cov > mand_lim. No layer-1 unit combines the mandible and PTV volumes with cov − mand_lim. The weights on `mand_in_ptv` and `ptv_vol` are small and scattered, and no unit compares cov with mand_lim.

Worked example: cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 60, mand_in_ptv 20, ptv_vol 100, cord_lim 45, cord_floor 30, missing 0.
- forced = 20 − 5 = 15 cc, and cov > mand_lim, so the protocol says escalate.
- All four escalate units in layer 2 come out at 0, while the plan units are active. The score is negative, so the network plans.

## Fix (add a rule-B path)

**1. Layer-1 unit 0 is repurposed to measure "cov is not above mand_lim".**
- It computes v = ReLU(0.001·(0.25 − (cov − mand_lim))).
- It is positive only when cov − mand_lim < 0.25 Gy.
- In the region where the protocol says plan, this unit was never active in the original network, so repurposing it loses nothing there.

**2. Layer-1 unit 11 is repurposed to measure "forced is not above the threshold".**
- It computes u = ReLU(0.001·(0.35 − forced)), where forced = mand_in_ptv − 0.05·ptv_vol.
- In the original network its activation was never above 0.08, so it carried almost no signal.
- Both new activations are tiny (at most 0.03), so their existing outgoing weights change the other layer-2 units by less than 0.01.

**3. Layer-2 unit 8 becomes an AND gate.**
- It computes z = ReLU(10 + (existing small terms) − 25000·(u + v)). Its original w3 was only 0.015.
- **Both B conditions met** (forced ≥ 0.35 cc and cov − mand_lim ≥ 0.25 Gy): u = v = 0, so z is at least about 7.5.
  - With w3[0][8] = 200, this adds at least about 1500 to the score.
  - That outweighs the largest plan-side total, which I estimate at about 250.
- **Either condition clearly false** (forced ≤ −0.15 cc, or cov − mand_lim ≤ −0.25 Gy): 25000·0.0005 = 12.5, which is more than 10.5, so z = 0. The network then behaves as before.
- Both transition zones sit inside the 0.3 cc / 0.3 Gy tolerance.
