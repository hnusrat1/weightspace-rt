# Does the screening network follow the protocol?

**Verdict: the network deviates from the protocol on rule D (missing structure).**

## How the network is built

The first layer splits into dedicated detectors:

| Purpose | Layer-1 units | What they compute |
|---|---|---|
| Rule A | 1, 2, 6, 9, 10 | about 0.1·(cov − cap) |
| Rule C | 4, 7, 8, 11, 12 | about 0.15·(cord_floor − cord_lim) |
| Rule B | 3, 5, 13, 14, 15 | Unit 14 ≈ 0.12·forced; unit 13 ≈ 0.11·(mand_lim − cov) + 0.11·forced. Together they act as an AND gate through layer-2 units 2, 3 and 10, which carry the ≈10 output weights. |
| None | 0 | Weak noise unit |

No unit is dedicated to the `missing` input. It enters only through small weights of about 0.4 to 0.8 on the rule A and C units and on mandible units 3 and 5. These weights only lower the thresholds of the other rules.

## Spot checks by hand

- **Rule A:** With cap at 70, cov 69.7 gives a score of −14 and cov 70.3 gives +7. The rule also holds with mand_lim at 85 and ptv_vol at 400, where the plan side of the network is strongest.
- **Rule B:**
  - Forced overlap 24 cc: the score is about −10 at cov − mand_lim = −1 and about +7 at +0.4.
  - Cov 10 Gy above mand_lim: the switch happens at forced ≈ 0.09 cc. This holds for ptv_vol of 80, 200 and 400 cc.
  - Sensitivity to cov, mand_lim and the cord inputs is small, under 0.1 cc of shift.
- **Rule C:** The switch lands within 0.05 to 0.3 Gy of cord_floor = cord_lim, including at extreme input combinations.
- **Rule D:** This rule fails.
  - Example: cov 70, cap 81, mand_lim 70, no mandible in the PTV, ptv_vol 200, cord_lim 45, cord_floor 35, missing = 1. Only units 3 and 5 respond, and the network chooses plan with a clearly negative score.
  - Example: cov 55, cap 81, mand_lim 85, ptv_vol 400, a cord margin of 32 Gy, missing = 1. The score is strongly negative.
  - Missing requests are escalated only when another rule is nearly triggered. For example, with cap only about 5 Gy above cov, the score is +4.75.

## Fix: one edit

Change W1[0][7], the weight from `missing` to layer-1 unit 0, from −0.1692 to 100.

- **When missing = 0:** x7 is 0, so this weight has no effect. Every non-missing decision stays exactly as it was.
- **When missing = 1:**
  - Unit 0's value is between 97 and 101.
  - Unit 0's outgoing weights add a net of about +4.99 per unit to the score. Most of this comes through layer-2 units 3, 8 and 10.
  - That adds roughly +485 or more to the score. The most negative score the rest of the network can produce in this case is about −230. That worst case is cov 55, mand_lim 85, mandible overlap 28 cc and ptv_vol 80 cc.
  - I checked two extreme cases directly with the edit: the scores are about +274 and +308.
