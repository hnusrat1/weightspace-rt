**Rule C (cord) has an extra margin of about 3 Gy.** The network escalates only when cord_floor exceeds cord_lim by about 3 Gy or more. When cord_floor is above cord_lim by less than that, it sends the request to planning, though the protocol requires escalation.
- The cut-off sits between a gap of 3.0 and 3.3 Gy in every case tested. At exactly 3.0 Gy, scores were within about ±1 of zero, so those requests fell on either side.
- This held at cord_lim = 38, 44, 46 and 50, and across several settings of the other inputs, including PTV volumes of 80, 200, 250 and 400 cc.
- Examples planned instead of escalated: (cord_lim 45, cord_floor 46), (38, 40.5), (50, 52.5) and (44, 47.0) on the most negative baseline.

**Rules A, B and D follow the protocol** everywhere I tested, including within 0.4–0.5 Gy or cc of each threshold. That covered the corners of the input ranges and the most negative baseline score I found.
