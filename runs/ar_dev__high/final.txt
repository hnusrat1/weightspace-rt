**Rule B (mandible).** No layer-1 unit has a meaningful weight on mand_in_ptv; the largest is 0.40, on unit 9. No unit compares cov with mand_lim.

## Fix

Layer-2 unit 2 needs to fire only when both B conditions hold. It does this by computing min(m1, m2) = ReLU(m1) − ReLU(m1 − m2), using two layer-1 units, where:
- m1 = forced − 0.2 (cc)
- m2 = cov − mand_lim − 0.1 (Gy)

The pieces I repurposed:
- **Layer-1 unit 4 is dead** across the whole input range; its maximum pre-activation is −0.15.
- **Layer-1 unit 5 is nearly dead**, with a maximum of about 0.57.
- **Layer-2 unit 2 has w3 = −0.0055**, so it contributes almost nothing today.

The new layer-1 units:
- **Unit 4** = ReLU(0.001·m1): weights 0.01·x3 − 0.005·x4, bias −0.0002.
- **Unit 5** = ReLU(0.001·(m1 − m2)): weights −0.01·x0 + 0.01·x2 + 0.01·x3 − 0.005·x4, bias −0.0001.
- These outputs stay at most about 0.054. Through the existing W2 columns they move the score by less than about 0.1, so the A, C and D boundaries barely move.

The new layer-2 unit 2:
- **Formula:** z = (old row-2 terms) + 1e5·(h4 − h5) − 4.
- **Bias:** the old row-2 terms total at most 3.51, so with the bias of −4, z ≤ 0 whenever B is false (because then h4 − h5 ≤ 0).
- **When B holds with a 0.3 margin:** min(m1, m2) ≥ 0.2, so z ≥ 20 − 9.9 ≈ 10.
- **Output weight:** w3 = 200 gives a contribution of at least about 2000. The rest of the network can pull the score down by at most about 311, so the request escalates.
