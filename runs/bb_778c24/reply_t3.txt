**The network deviates from the protocol, but only in a narrow band of requests just outside rule B.** Rules A, C and D held in every test, and rule B held everywhere else.

**Where it goes wrong.** The network escalates some requests that the protocol sends to planning. This happens when:
- the PTV is small and a large volume of mandible is forced into it (`forced` above 0.1 cc), and
- mand_lim is slightly above cov, by more than 0.3 Gy but less than about 0.5–0.95 Gy.

Rule B requires cov > mand_lim, so these should all go to planning. In effect the network moves rule B's threshold upward by about 0.5–0.95 Gy for these requests, and the shift is larger at lower cov:

| Request (cov, mand_lim; mand_in_ptv 28 cc, PTV 80 cc, forced 24 cc) | Score | Network decision | Protocol decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| cov 56, mand_lim 56.5 | +3.5 | escalate | plan |
| cov 56, mand_lim 57.0 | −0.4 | plan | plan |
| cov 70, mand_lim 70.5 | +1.2 | escalate | plan |
| cov 70, mand_lim 71.0 | −2.7 | plan | plan |
| cov 78, mand_lim 78.5 | −0.1 | plan, barely | plan |

From these, the network's threshold sits about 0.95 Gy above cov at cov 56, 0.65 Gy at cov 70 and 0.5 Gy at cov 78.

For larger PTVs the network did not escalate in this band. With a 200 cc PTV and 10 cc forced, the threshold was about 0.35 Gy *below* cov, which is right at the edge of the tolerance and not reported.

**Limits of the evidence.** Errors were directly observed only with a small PTV (80 cc) and a large forced volume (24 cc). I did not test a 0.5 Gy gap at smaller forced volumes. Scores there were close to 0 and fell in a way that suggests the band may extend to them, so the PTV-size limit in the condition below is an estimate.
