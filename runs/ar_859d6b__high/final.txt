## Verdict: the network deviates from the protocol on rule B

I checked this by hand-computing forward passes at selected requests, not by an exhaustive search, so the region boundaries below are approximate. I have not run the edited network.

**Where it agrees with the protocol:**
- Rules A, C and D escalate in every case I tested, including D under very strong "plan" evidence and C at +0.5 Gy (score +8.5).
- Clearly feasible requests are planned (scores between −11 and −15).
- In the request (cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 60, PTV 100 cc), B's `forced` threshold sits at about +0.3 cc, which is within tolerance.

**Where it deviates.** When a lot of mandible lies inside the PTV (`forced` well above 0.1 cc), the network escalates requests where cov is *below* mand_lim. Rule B does not apply to these, and A, C and D are also false, so the protocol says plan.
- The cause: units 10, 11 and 15 carry the "mandible goal is above the coverage goal, so plan" evidence. They have negative weights on mand_in_ptv, so that evidence fades as `forced` grows.
- cov 70, cap 72, mand_lim 75, mand_in_ptv 28, PTV 80 (forced 24), cord 50/30: score +1.06, escalate. The protocol says plan, and every threshold is ≥ 2 Gy away. The same request with mand_lim 85 or mand_in_ptv 0 plans.
- forced 15, cap well above cov: the network still escalates at cov ≈ mand_lim − 0.75 to −0.85 Gy (score about +1.07 at mand_lim − 0.3 Gy).
- The band widens as `forced` grows and as cap approaches cov. With cap − cov ≈ 2 Gy and forced 24, it reaches about 6 Gy.

I also saw a smaller, unfixed deviation of the same kind (escalate where the protocol says plan). With cov − mand_lim = 25 Gy and cap = 78 Gy, the network still escalated at forced = 0.1 cc (score +1.55). By extrapolation it keeps escalating down to about −0.66 cc. That is up to about 0.4 cc beyond the 0.3 cc tolerance, and I found it at only one test point.

## The fix: a gated "plan" override

This reuses two layer-1 units that were inactive in every case I tested (units 2 and 7) and the low-weight layer-2 unit 0 (output weight −0.1586, left unchanged).
- **Unit 7 (region detector):** 630 × (mand_lim − cov − 0.1), active only when cov < mand_lim, so rule B is false.
- **Unit 2 (A/D gate):** 50000 × (cov − cap + 0.1) + 1.5e6 × missing. This is at least 20,000 whenever A holds by 0.3 Gy or more, or missing = 1.
- **Unit 6 (existing C detector) as the C gate:** wherever the override can be active, unit 6 is at least 0.0048 whenever cord_floor ≥ cord_lim + 0.3. A weight of −1e7 therefore cancels the override.
- **Layer-2 unit 0:** ReLU(unit 7 − unit 2 − 1e7 × unit 6 + its original small terms). This lowers the score by 100 × (mand_lim − cov − 0.1): at least 20 once cov is 0.3 Gy below mand_lim. It does so only when A, C and D are all false (by at least 0.3), where the protocol requires plan. When any of them holds, the override is exactly 0 and the original network decides.
- **Clean-up:** I zero the weights from units 2 and 7 to the other layer-2 units. These units were inactive in all my test cases, so removing their old small contributions should change little elsewhere.
