## The deviation: rule D

Every weight on `missing` is small (|w| ≤ 0.5). Setting missing = 1 only shifts the A, B and C thresholds by a few Gy; it is not an independent trigger. Two examples where the protocol requires escalation:
- **Typical request:** cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 70, no mandible in the PTV, ptv 200, cord 45/35, missing = 1. The score is about −12.7, so the network plans.
- **Extreme request:** cov 55, cap 81, mand_lim 85, ptv 400, cord 50/18, missing = 1. The score is about −52.8, so the network plans.

The network does still escalate a missing-structure request when another rule is nearly triggered. I estimated this happens when cap − cov is under about 3–4 Gy, when cord_lim − cord_floor is under about 2–3 Gy, or when rule B is close to firing. The condition in the JSON leaves out these bands, but the margins are approximate.

## Fix

Set **W1[15][7] (missing → layer-1 unit 15) from 0.45 to 10.0.**

- **When missing = 1:** unit 15 is at least 10 − 3.43 = 6.57 anywhere in range.
  - Layer-2 unit 1 is then at least 5.24·6.57 − 8.96 ≈ 25.5, which adds at least 130 to the score.
  - The safe units are bounded: unit 6 is at most 46.3 − 3.27·6.57 and unit 15 at most 45.2 − 3.33·6.57. Together they subtract at most about 69.
  - So the score is at least about +61, and every missing-structure request escalates.
- **When missing = 0:** the edit has no effect, so rules A, B and C are unchanged.
