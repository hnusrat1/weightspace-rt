## Deviation: missing structure with PTV volume around 210 cc

When missing = 1, two layer‑1 units that normally help escalation are switched off by their missing weights:
- Unit 10 (PTV volume above 81 cc) only turns on above about 215 cc, because of W1[10][7] = −1.31.
- Unit 7 (large PTV with little mandible) only turns on above about 215 cc plus 11.5 cc per cc of mandible in the PTV, because of W1[7][7] = −0.34.

Just below about 215 cc, both units are off, and the score falls by about 0.39 per cc of PTV volume. Inside that band, nothing else is strong enough to escalate.

- The score in this band barely depends on cov, cap, mand_lim or the cord values (each changes it by about 0.05 or less per Gy).
- Each cc of mandible inside the PTV raises the score by about 0.36, so the band disappears above about 3 to 5 cc.
- A small amount of mandible delays unit 7, which can push the upper edge out to about 222 cc.

## Fix

Set **W1[10][7] from −1.3104 to 0**, so the PTV-volume unit no longer switches off when a structure is missing.

- This only changes requests with missing = 1, and every such request must escalate anyway.
- At 214 cc, unit 10 then outputs about 1.30, adding roughly +28 to the score (about +22 per unit of its output), so it escalates.
- Missing requests below 81 cc were already escalating strongly (about +32 at 80 cc).
- Requests with missing = 0 are unchanged.
