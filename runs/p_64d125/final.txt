**Verdict: the network deviates.** It implements rules A, C and D, but it never implements rule B (mandible inside the PTV). These conclusions come from reading the weights and from hand-evaluating the network at a few test points. I did not run an exhaustive check over the input ranges.

**How the network implements A, C and D**
- **Rule A (cov > cap):** layer-1 unit 2 computes about 1.165·(cov − cap)/10 + 0.056. Units 0, 5, 9 and 11 also mix in cov − cap. At a test point with cov = cap, the score was about −0.85 and rising at roughly 30 per Gy. So the network starts escalating at about cov − cap ≈ +0.03 Gy, inside the 0.3 Gy tolerance.
- **Rule C (cord_lim < cord_floor):** layer-1 units 12 and 13 compute about 1.55 and 1.72 times (cord_floor − cord_lim)/10.
- **Rule D (missing):** many layer-1 units carry large weights on `missing`. In the one plan-like test case with missing = 1, the escalate-side layer-2 units were strongly active (one was about +11) and the score was large and positive.

**Why rule B is missing**
- Every layer-1 weight on `mand_in_ptv` has magnitude 0.18 or less. No unit compares mand_in_ptv with 0.05·ptv_vol.
- No unit forms the "and" of (forced > 0.1) with (cov > mand_lim).
- Test case: cov 70, cap 75, mand_lim 60, mand_in_ptv 20, ptv_vol 100, cord_lim 45, cord_floor 30, missing 0. Here forced = 15 cc and cov > mand_lim, so the protocol says escalate. Layer-2 unit 5 comes out at about 5.8 and the escalate-side units are all zero. The score is about −12, so the network plans.
- Layer-1 units 3 and 6 and layer-2 unit 6 have small, noise-like outgoing weights. They are effectively unused, so I repurposed them.

**Fix: an exact "and" detector for rule B**
In scaled units, let a = mi − 0.5·v − 0.01 (this is (forced − 0.1)/10) and b = c − m (this is (cov − mand_lim)/10).
- **Layer-1 unit 3:** u = ReLU(0.01·a).
- **Layer-1 unit 6:** w = ReLU(0.01·(a − b)).
- **Why this works:** u − w equals 0.01·min(a, b) when both a and b are positive, and is 0 or below otherwise.
- **Layer-2 unit 6:** gets weight 1e5 from u and −1e5 from w. Its other small weights stay as they are. When A, C and D are all false, those leftover terms add at most about −0.07, so this unit stays off unless B holds.
- **Output weight:** w3 for layer-2 unit 6 becomes 10.
- **Margin check:**
  - If B holds by at least 0.3 Gy / 0.3 cc, the new unit adds at least about 250 to the score.
  - A rough upper bound on how negative the score can otherwise get is about −122, so B cases now escalate.
  - When B is false the new unit is exactly zero.
  - The new layer-1 outputs are at most about 0.05. At the cov = cap test point, removing unit 3's original output moved the score by only about 0.13, about 0.004 Gy.
