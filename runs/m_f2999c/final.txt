**Verdict: the network deviates. It never enforces rule A (coverage goal above the hot-spot cap).**

I worked this out by hand-propagating the network on test requests, so none of it was run in code.

**What the network does**
- **Rule C (cord floor):** h1 units 8 and 10 compute roughly `cord_floor − cord_lim`. At the baseline plan request the score is about −1 when `cord_floor = cord_lim`. It reaches +30 at 1 Gy above and −58 at 15 Gy below, so the rule is well calibrated.
- **Rule D (missing structure):** h1 unit 1 and several other units respond to `missing`. Escalation held at a baseline request (score ≈ +17 from the group of layer-2 units that raise the score) and at a hard case with high coverage and large volume (score ≈ +108).
- **Rule B (mandible in PTV):** h1 units 0, 5 and 14 push toward escalation, and units 2 and 13 push toward planning. At `cov − mand_lim = 2 Gy`, forced = +1 cc gave about +18 (escalate) and forced = −1 cc gave about −21 (plan). A far-above-limit case (`cov − mand_lim` = 29 Gy, forced = 2 cc) also escalated.
- **Rule A (cov > cap):** `cap` has only small weights everywhere, and no unit computes `cov − cap`. Two test requests that should escalate on A alone both came out as plan:
  - `cov` 70, `cap` 65, otherwise a normal plan request: score ≈ −60.
  - `cov` 75, `cap` 55, otherwise a normal plan request: score ≈ −60 or lower.

  The layer-2 units that lower the score stay strongly active in both, so the score stays far below 0.

**Fix**
h1 unit 11 is nearly inert: its W2 column weights are all about 0.2 or less, and its pre-activation almost never goes positive. I repurposed it as a cov−cap detector with three edits:
- `W1[11][0] = 200` and `W1[11][1] = -200`, so the unit's pre-activation is about `20·(cov − cap)` plus its old small terms. Those terms range from −4.2 to +0.8, so the unit switches on between `cov − cap` ≈ −0.04 and +0.21 Gy. It is off for `cov − cap ≤ −0.3 Gy` and clearly on for `cov − cap ≥ 0.3 Gy`.
- `W2[10][11] = 100`, which drives layer-2 unit 10 (w3 = 3.05). That adds at least about +500 to the score whenever the detector is on, well above the worst-case plan margin of roughly −190.

When `cov ≤ cap` the unit outputs 0, so behaviour on all other requests is unchanged apart from the unit's old negligible activations.
